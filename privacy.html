Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

About this policy

This policy explains how information may be handled when you use Adams, a baseball-inspired app intended for people aged 17 and older. Available features and how they store information may vary by version.

Information you provide

If you use email sign-in, we process your email address and authentication information. If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple may provide an account identifier and email address. Profile details you choose to add may include a name, photo, city and biography. Depending on the features you use, information may also include photos, videos, notes, comments, likes, follows, blocks, reports and messages.

How information is used

Information is used to provide the features you choose, maintain access, save your activity, support safety and address problems. Some information is stored on your device; account, content or interaction information may also be stored by service providers when an online feature is used. Network and device information, including IP address and service logs, may be processed for delivery, security and troubleshooting.

Purchases and sharing

Apple processes purchases under its own terms and privacy practices. Purchase identifiers may be retained to deliver purchases and prevent duplicate delivery; Adams does not receive your full payment-card number. When an online sharing feature is available and you choose to use it, your profile and shared content may be visible to other people. Service providers may process information to operate the app, and information may be disclosed where required by law or necessary for security or legal claims.

Your choices

You can choose whether to grant camera, microphone and photo access in iOS settings. You can edit available profile details and use the controls provided to manage your content or account. Device-only data may be lost when the app is deleted, the device is lost or its data is cleared. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information, subject to applicable legal and transaction-record obligations.

Storage and changes

Information is retained as needed for the features you use and for legal, security and transaction-record purposes. Information handled by service providers may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. We use safeguards appropriate to the service, but no system can guarantee absolute security. We may update this policy and revise the date above.
